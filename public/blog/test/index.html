Team Dance Illusions Win 32 Medals at India International Tanzsport Contesa 2026

October 2, 2026

Goa’s Team Dance Illusions, coached by Dr. Martin D’Costa, returned from the India International Tanzsport Contesa 2026 in Mumbai with an impressive haul of 32 medals: 13 Gold, 9 Silver and 10 Bronze.

Medal tally

  • 🥇 13 Gold
  • 🥈 9 Silver
  • 🥉 10 Bronze
  • 🏅 32 medals in total

Two standout stories

Ten-year-old Katriel Fernandes competed in the Below-14 category and claimed Gold in Jive and Silver in Samba, becoming the youngest medallist of the contingent.

At the other end of the age spectrum, our senior dancers delivered a dominant performance in the Above-35 Latin events. Ajit Mishra & Priyanka Ojha swept Gold in Cha Cha, Samba and Jive, while Avinash Tavares & Rati Pai Angle took the Rumba Gold, together securing every Latin Gold medal available in the Above-35 category.
